🛡 BUTTON GUIDE

CONTROL BUTTONS

MODE: SIMPLE Toggle between SIMPLE (no notifications) and ALERT (sends Telegram notifications when person detected)
RESET THREAT Reset threat level back to SAFE
RADAR ON/OFF Toggle motion radar visualization (shows detected motion points)
NETWORK MAP Show network topology of cameras and devices
CLEAR LOG Clear all alerts from the feed

FILTER & STREAM BUTTONS

ALL / MED / HIGH Filter alerts by threat level (All, Medium, or High only)
PAUSE STREAM / RESUME STREAM Pause or resume live camera feeds
SOUND ON / SOUND OFF Enable or disable alert notification sounds

CAPTURE & EXPORT

SNAPSHOT Capture current frame from active camera
EXPORT CSV Download all alerts as CSV file for analysis

TIPS

  • Click any camera to expand full-screen view
  • Press Spacebar to pause/resume stream
  • Press 'S' key to capture snapshot
  • Press 'E' key to export CSV
  • Use filters to focus on important alerts
